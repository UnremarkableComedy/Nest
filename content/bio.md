Dylan Mercer is a stand-up comedian whose performances blend sharp observation with a measured, conversational delivery. His material draws from everyday life, the American South, and the quiet absurdities of modern existence.

Based in Asheville, North Carolina, Dylan has performed at clubs and independent venues across the country. His work has been featured on national comedy platforms and live showcase programs, and he tours regularly throughout the year.

His stage presence is unhurried and deliberate, favoring precision over volume. Each set is built with the care of a craftsman — shaped, refined, and delivered with the confidence of a performer who trusts the material.
